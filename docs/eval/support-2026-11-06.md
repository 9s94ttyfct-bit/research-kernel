The raw τ_hi_judged (0.7950) is above τ_hi, so the engine applies min(τ_hi, τ_hi_judged) = 0.5565 when two judges
agree. Bespoke's p is far better spread than Flan-T5's, so layer 1 can decide on its own (dev coverage 0.77 instead of
0.10), but at τ_hi its held-out precision (0.927) is below the 0.95 target. That is why a "supported" verdict still
needs two agreeing judges. With the judges in the loop, both models give almost the same result; the remaining gap to
70 % coverage is judge disagreement and "partial" answers, not layer 1.
